The simulations use the Adams–Bashforth–Moulton predictor–corrector scheme (Diethelm, Ford, Freed) for the Caputo derivative, with step h = 0.005 over t from 0 to 15. A lock counts as open when the error norm stays below 0.01 for the rest of the run. Settling times are measured on these runs and are not analytical bounds. The generated Python reproduces the numbers on this page up to floating-point rounding.
